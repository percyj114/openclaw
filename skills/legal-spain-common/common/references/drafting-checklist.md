# Drafting Checklist

Use this when the user asks for a draft, review, clause mark-up, or demand letter.

## Review workflow

1. Identify the document type.
2. Identify the commercial objective.
3. Flag the top three legal risks first.
4. Distinguish unusual clauses from merely unfavorable clauses.
5. Suggest fixes in business terms first, legal wording second.

## Drafting workflow

1. State assumptions.
2. Keep placeholders in brackets if facts are missing.
3. Use short clauses and direct language.
4. Add only the terms needed for the stated use case.
5. Do not over-lawyer first drafts.

## Demand-letter workflow

1. State the contract or invoice basis.
2. State the breach or non-performance clearly.
3. State what cure is required and by when.
4. Reserve rights without making unsupported threats.
5. Keep escalation language proportionate.
