# Investment Structuring Question Map

## Q15

Asset-management SL, management fees, and carried interest.

First sources:

- Corporate Income Tax Law
- Law 28/2022
- DGT rulings on carried interest and fees
- Companies Act

## Q16

Tax impact of keeping an inactive SL dormant versus dissolving it.

First sources:

- Companies Act
- Corporate Income Tax Law
- DGT rulings on dissolution and liquidation distributions

## Q19

Spanish SL paying interest to a Luxembourg vehicle.

First sources:

- Domestic withholding rules
- Spain-Luxembourg treaty
- AEAT non-resident guidance

## Q21

Spanish SL issuing convertible notes to foreign investors.

First sources:

- Companies Act
- Law 6/2023
- EU Prospectus Regulation
- CNMV guidance

## Q30

Basic shareholders' agreement for two founders with different contribution types.

First sources:

- Companies Act
- market-form terms to collect into a drafting note

## Common missing facts

- Investor profile and count
- Whether the offer is private or public
- Whether there is a foreign holding stack
- Founder vesting expectations
- Whether management fees are intra-group or third-party
