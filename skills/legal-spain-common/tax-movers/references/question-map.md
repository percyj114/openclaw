# Tax Movers Question Map

## Q11

Moved from London to Spain in 2024, UK rental income, US brokerage account, Modelo 720.

First sources:

- AEAT Modelo 720 procedure page
- AEAT filing-window page
- Law 35/2006
- Spain-UK treaty
- Spain-US treaty

## Q12

Beckham eligibility after prior Spanish residence from 2005 to 2012.

First sources:

- Law 35/2006 article 93
- Royal Decree 439/2007
- AEAT special regime page
- DGT rulings on prior residence

## Q13

Covered-call premiums on US equities while resident in Spain.

First sources:

- Law 35/2006 rules on savings-base income and capital gains
- DGT rulings on options and derivatives
- Spain-US treaty only if source allocation becomes relevant

## Q14

Managing a non-resident family member's portfolio from Spain.

First sources:

- Law 35/2006 residence rules
- Spain-Argentina treaty
- DGT rulings on representation, management, agency, and beneficial ownership

## Q17

Around 100 days per year in the UK after moving to Spain.

First sources:

- Spanish residence rules in Law 35/2006
- Spain-UK treaty tie-break rules
- HMRC RDR3 guidance

## Common missing facts

- Exact arrival and departure dates
- Family location
- Main home availability
- Employer structure
- Account ownership and signatory rights
- Whether trading is personal, discretionary, or under power of attorney
