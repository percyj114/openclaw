# Catalonia Property Corpus Index

## Scope

Use this corpus for:

- Questions 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29
- Montseny and rural property
- Catalan planning and protected-land rules
- Barcelona short-term rental regulation
- vehicle import, homologation, and matriculation
- volunteer hosting risk
- patrimonio protegido
- property-side drafting and contractor disputes

3. Portal Juridic de Catalunya
   https://portaljuridic.gencat.cat/ca/

4. DOGC search
   https://dogc.gencat.cat/ca/serveis/Consulta-del-DOGC/

5. CIDO local-regulation portal

   https://www.sedecatastro.gob.es/

    https://sede.dgt.gob.es/

    Official source: BOE search for `Ley 45/2015 Voluntariado`
    Root: https://www.boe.es/

13. Occupational-risk law, Law 31/1995
    Official source: BOE search for `Ley 31/1995 Prevención de Riesgos Laborales`

14. Catalan volunteering law, Law 25/2015
    Official sources: Portal Juridic and DOGC
    Roots: https://portaljuridic.gencat.cat/ca/ , https://dogc.gencat.cat/ca/serveis/Consulta-del-DOGC/

15. Law 41/2003 on patrimonio protegido
    Official source: BOE search for `Ley 41/2003 patrimonio protegido`
    Root: https://www.boe.es/

16. Customs and import guidance for post-Brexit vehicle import
    Official source: AEAT customs portal
    Root: https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/
