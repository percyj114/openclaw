# Spain Credit Regulatory

Start by reading:

- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/common/references/answer-contract.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/credit-regulatory/references/corpus-index.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/credit-regulatory/references/question-map.md`

Method:

1. Identify the actual activity:
   - direct lending
   - buying receivables
   - factoring
   - brokering
   - servicing
   - fund-raising for credit exposure
2. Identify who originates, who owns, who services, who bears risk, and who faces the borrower.
3. Answer perimeter questions in this order:
   - what is clearly being done
   - which regulator could care
   - what is clearly outside perimeter
   - what is gray
   - which facts change the answer

For receivables assignments:

- Check assignability restrictions.
- Check debtor notice and proof mechanics.
- Check debtor defenses and set-off risk.
- Check data and confidentiality restrictions.
- Check insolvency sensitivity.

For security packages:

- Explain market-standard choices by collateral type.
- Favor enforceability and market practice over theory.

Special traps:

- Unauthorized regulated activity by accident
- Platform doing more than a pure technology role
- Assignment language that blocks a true transfer
- Consumer-credit spillover
- Security that looks broad but is weak in enforcement
