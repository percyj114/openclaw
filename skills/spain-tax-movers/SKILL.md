# Spain Tax Movers

Start by reading:

- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/common/references/answer-contract.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/tax-movers/references/corpus-index.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/tax-movers/references/question-map.md`

Issue buckets:

- Residency
- Special impatriate regime
- Foreign asset reporting
- Income characterization
- Treaty allocation
- Agency or permanent-establishment risk
- Wealth tax or solidarity tax spillover

Method:

1. Classify the question into one or two issue buckets.
2. Apply Spanish domestic law first.
3. Then apply treaty rules if residence or source overlap matters.
4. Then check official doctrine or guidance.
5. Separate legal ownership, beneficial ownership, control, and place of management.

For reporting questions:

- Identify who files.
- Identify which assets are in scope.
- State thresholds and refiling triggers only if sourced.
- State the filing window with exact dates when relevant.

For Beckham questions:

- Test each eligibility element one by one.
- Flag any weak point separately.
- Treat prior Spanish residence as a red-flag fact, not a footnote.

For income characterization questions:

- Name the competing tax treatments.
- Explain which treatment is more defensible.
- Say what facts could flip the answer.

For UK overlap:

- Separate Spanish residence risk from HMRC statutory residence test risk.
- Do not assume the UK position from Spanish facts alone.

Special traps:

- Split-year assumptions
- Prior Spanish residence history
- Joint or nominee accounts
- Options or derivatives classification
- Portfolio management performed from Spain
- Foreign reporting obligations confused with taxability
