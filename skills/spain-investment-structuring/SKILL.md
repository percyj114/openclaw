# Spain Investment Structuring

Start by reading:

- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/common/references/answer-contract.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/investment-structuring/references/corpus-index.md`
- `{baseDir}/../legal-spain-common/investment-structuring/references/question-map.md`

Method:

1. Identify the structure under review:
   - operating SL
   - management company
   - investment vehicle
   - finance instrument
   - liquidation or dormancy
2. Separate:
   - corporate law
   - tax
   - securities or regulatory perimeter
   - accounting or execution burden
3. If the user is comparing options, use a side-by-side format.

For outbound payments:

- State the domestic rule first.
- Then flag treaty relief possibilities.
- Then flag beneficial-owner, anti-abuse, and substance risks.

For convertibles:

- Check governance and approvals.
- Check dilution and pre-emption mechanics.
- Check prospectus perimeter.
- Check foreign investor friction points.

For dormant versus dissolution:

- Compare compliance burden, cost, director exposure, tax filings, and exit cleanliness.

Special traps:

- Related-party pricing
- Management-fee VAT or transfer-pricing issues
- Carried-interest characterization risk
- Substance issues in foreign vehicles
- Treaty shopping
- Prospectus assumptions based on labels instead of actual offer mechanics
